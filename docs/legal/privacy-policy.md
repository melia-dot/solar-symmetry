# Solar Symmetry: Privacy policy

**Short version:** Solar Symmetry works on your phone. Your city, your settings and your sun times stay on your phone. We don't have accounts, we don't show ads, and we don't sell anything about you. Anonymous usage stats are only collected if you say Yes, and you can switch them off any time.

## Who we are

Solar Symmetry ("the app") is made by [YOUR FULL NAME], an independent developer in the United Kingdom. For UK data protection law, I am the "controller" of the small amount of personal data described below.

Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL]

## What stays on your phone

These are saved only on your phone and never sent to us:

- the city you choose (and the two cities in Compare Cities)
- your settings: which companion messages you want, and your Yes or No to usage stats
- your sunrise, sunset and daylight times, which the app calculates on your phone

Companion messages are created and scheduled on your phone too. Deleting the app deletes all of this.

## City search and "Use my location"

To find a city, the app sends what you type to **Nominatim**, the place search service run by the OpenStreetMap Foundation. Like any website or online service, Nominatim also receives your phone's internet (IP) address. The app only searches when you press Search. See the [OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy).

If you tap **Use my location**, your phone asks whether Solar Symmetry may use your location. If you allow it, the app reads your position once, **rounds it to about 1 km**, and sends only that rounded position to Nominatim to find the name of your town. Only the rounded position and the town name are saved, on your phone. The app never tracks your location in the background, and we never receive it.

## Anonymous usage stats (only if you say Yes)

When you first open the app, we ask: "Help improve Solar Symmetry by sharing anonymous usage stats?" Nothing is collected unless you tap **Yes**. You can change your answer at any time in **Settings → Share anonymous usage stats**.

If you say Yes, the app uses **Google Analytics for Firebase**, provided by Google, to count things like:

- which screens are opened and when the app is used
- events such as finishing the welcome screens, choosing a city (not which city), turning on messages, or buying the unlock
- your app version, phone model, operating system and language
- an approximate country or region, which Google works out from your internet address (your internet address itself is not stored)
- a random analytics ID for this copy of the app, so visits can be counted

We **don't** collect your name, email, your city or your exact location, and we don't use advertising IDs. We don't use the stats for advertising and Google may not use them for advertising either: we switch off Google's ad features and data sharing.

- **Why:** to see which parts of the app are useful and improve them.
- **Legal basis:** your consent (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(a), and the consent rules in the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations).
- **Who handles it:** Google acts as our processor, which means it may only use the data on our instructions. Google may process it outside the UK, including in the United States, under safeguards recognised by UK law (the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and/or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum).
- **How long:** the stats are kept for 2 months, then deleted by Google. We may keep overall totals (such as "500 people opened the app in December") that can't identify anyone.
- **Changing your mind:** switch it off in Settings. The app then stops sending stats straight away, resets the analytics ID on your phone, and asks Google to delete the stats linked to that ID.

## Buying the Companion unlock

The one-time unlock is sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play. They handle the payment under their own privacy policies; we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns whether the unlock has been bought on your account, so it can switch the features on and restore them if you reinstall.

## Notifications

If you turn on companion messages, your phone asks for permission to show notifications. The messages are created on your phone from text built into the app. You can change which messages you get in Settings, or turn notifications off in your phone's Settings.

## Children

Solar Symmetry is not designed for children under 13.

## Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

- ask what personal data we hold about you and get a copy
- ask us to correct or delete it
- object to or restrict how we use it
- withdraw your consent at any time (for usage stats: the switch in Settings)

Because almost everything stays on your phone, we usually hold nothing that identifies you. To use any of these rights, email [CONTACT EMAIL].

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, please tell us first. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): [ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/), phone 0303 123 1113.
